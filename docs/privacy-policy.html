BlastDown Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026
Last updated: September 16, 2026
App: BlastDown (com.blastdown.app)
Developer: Naxum Games

Summary: BlastDown does not require an account and does not operate its own server for storing player profiles or gameplay data. Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. BlastDown includes optional rewarded advertising provided by Google Mobile Ads, which may process certain device, usage, advertising, and diagnostic information.

1. Information stored on your device

BlastDown stores information needed to provide and maintain your game experience in the app's private Android storage. This may include:

  • Your active game and board state
  • Your best score
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Sound and music preferences
  • Haptics preferences
  • Reduced-motion preferences

BlastDown does not currently provide user accounts, login functionality, cloud saves, user-generated content, or a BlastDown-operated online backend.

This locally stored gameplay and preference data is not uploaded by BlastDown. It remains on your device until it is replaced through normal app use, you clear the app's storage, or you uninstall the app.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

BlastDown offers optional rewarded advertisements for certain gameplay features, including Freeze and Defuse.

Advertising is provided through Google Mobile Ads, and privacy/consent choices may be managed using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

When Google Mobile Ads is used, Google's SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a user's general or approximate location
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad impressions, and video views
  • Diagnostic, performance, and crash-related information associated with the ad SDK
  • Device or advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID or App Set ID when available
  • Information used for advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention

Google controls how information processed through Google Mobile Ads is stored and retained within Google's systems.

BlastDown does not receive or maintain a copy of Google's advertising profile about you.

3. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable law, BlastDown uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent.

BlastDown checks Google's consent status before eligible advertising services are initialized or ads are requested.

If consent cannot be obtained, consent information cannot be resolved, or an advertisement is otherwise unavailable, the rewarded-ad feature may be unavailable. This does not prevent you from playing the core game.

Where Google's privacy-options form is required, it can be accessed through BlastDown's Privacy & Ad Choices interface.

You may also manage or reset your Android Advertising ID through your Android device settings where supported.

4. Offline gameplay

BlastDown's core gameplay can be played without an internet connection. An internet connection is required for optional advertising and related Google privacy services.

5. Third-party services

The primary production third-party service that may receive information from BlastDown is Google Mobile Ads, including Google's User Messaging Platform.

BlastDown does not currently include a separate third-party analytics service or third-party crash-reporting SDK.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

6. Data sharing

BlastDown does not sell your personal information.

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads may be shared with or processed by Google and its advertising technology partners according to Google's policies, your consent choices, applicable law, and the advertising configuration used for your region.

7. Data security

BlastDown limits its own stored information to gameplay continuity and user preferences. Local information is stored using Android's app-private storage mechanisms.

Network communications handled by Google Mobile Ads and UMP use encrypted transport as described by Google.

No method of electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable technical safeguards are used to reduce risk.

8. Data retention and deletion

BlastDown does not maintain user accounts or a server-side player database.

To delete information stored locally by BlastDown, you can:

  • Clear BlastDown's app storage through Android Settings, or
  • Uninstall BlastDown from your device.

Advertising and consent-related information processed by Google is governed by Google's own retention and deletion practices.

9. Children's privacy

BlastDown is not specifically designed or directed toward young children and does not knowingly request children to provide direct personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, or account profile.

Advertising and privacy behavior may vary based on applicable law, region, age-related settings, and Google Play or Google Mobile Ads requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us at the address below so that we can review the matter.

10. International users

BlastDown may be made available to users in multiple countries. Privacy and advertising requirements may differ by region. Where required, Google's consent tools are used before eligible advertising services are made available.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when BlastDown's features, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be posted at this page and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.
