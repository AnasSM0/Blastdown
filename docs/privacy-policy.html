Information stored on your device

BlastDown stores your active game, best score, tutorial status, and sound, music, haptics, and reduced-motion settings in the app's private Android storage. BlastDown has no account, login, cloud save, user-generated content, or BlastDown-operated backend. This local data is not uploaded by BlastDown and remains until replaced through normal use, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled.

Optional rewarded advertising

Optional Freeze and Defuse ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Core gameplay works offline and without ads. When an ad is requested, Google's SDK automatically collects and shares an IP address (which may estimate general location), product interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers including Android advertising ID and app set ID where available. Google states these data are used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and encrypted in transit using TLS.

BlastDown checks UMP before initializing or loading ads and requests non-personalized/limited inventory. If consent cannot be resolved or ads are ineligible, the optional ad is unavailable and gameplay continues.

Consent and choices

Where required, UMP presents Google's consent message and remains authoritative for consent state. BlastDown's in-app Privacy & Ad Choices screen can reopen Google's privacy-options form when Google requires it. Android advertising-ID controls are available in Android Settings.

Third-party service and retention

The only production service receiving data from the app is Google Mobile Ads, including UMP. BlastDown currently has no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google controls retention in its advertising systems; BlastDown does not receive or retain Google's advertising profile or consent record.

Security

Local game data is kept in Android app-private storage. Google Mobile Ads/UMP uses encrypted transport. BlastDown limits its own stored data to game continuity and preferences.

Children

BlastDown is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly ask for direct personal information. The owner must approve final Play target ages. Choosing a child-directed group requires a Families-policy, advertising, consent, declaration, and policy review before publication.

Deletion and contact

There is no BlastDown account to delete. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove local data. Reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings; Google handles data it processes.
